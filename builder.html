1Section 1 — Scope and Parties

Agreement header & clinical-discretion statement

S1-FIXED-HEADER

CONTROLLED SUBSTANCE PRESCRIBING AGREEMENT

This agreement is entered into between [PROVIDER NAME], [TITLE], of Crystal Run Healthcare, and [PATIENT NAME] (Date of Birth: [DOB]) on [DATE]. This agreement covers the following medication(s): [MEDICATION, DOSE, FREQUENCY].

This agreement is a clinical document establishing shared expectations for the prescribing relationship. It is advisory in nature; experienced providers retain clinical discretion. Nothing in this agreement limits the provider's ability to exercise independent clinical judgment.

Always included
Authority: CDC 2022 Recommendation 5 (establish treatment goals and care plan at inherited-patient first visit); ASAM/ACMT 2025 (pretaper assessment framework) 12
Grounded in statute / guideline

Indication — established

S1-VAR-INDICATION-ESTABLISHED

Indication: This agreement covers [MEDICATION] prescribed for the management of [DIAGNOSED CONDITION], documented in the medical record.

Included when Indication = established
Authority: Standard documentation practice
Grounded in statute / guideline

Indication — inherited / presumptive

S1-VAR-INDICATION-INHERITED

Indication: This agreement covers [MEDICATION] as an inherited prescription. A clinical assessment to formally establish the indication is planned within [N] visits. Continuation at this time is to prevent precipitated withdrawal or abrupt discontinuation harm pending that assessment. This continuation does not constitute endorsement of the prior prescribing provider's clinical decisions.

Included when Indication = inherited-presumptive
Authority: CDC 2022 Recommendation 5; ASAM/ACMT 2025 Section on Inherited Patients 12
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Indication — none, bridge supply

S1-VAR-INDICATION-NONE

Indication: No documented clinical indication has been established for [MEDICATION] at this time. This agreement covers a bridge supply only, issued to prevent precipitated withdrawal while a full clinical assessment is completed within [N] visits. Continuation beyond that assessment requires an established indication.

Included when Indication = none-pending-assessment
Authority: ASAM/ACMT 2025; institutional risk management 2
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

2Section 2 — Patient Obligations

Single prescriber

S2-FIXED-SINGLE-PRESCRIBER

Single prescriber: I agree to obtain [MEDICATION CLASS] prescriptions from a single prescriber — the provider named in this agreement. I will not seek prescriptions for [MEDICATION CLASS] from any other provider without the knowledge and documented coordination of my prescribing provider.

Always included
Authority: 21 CFR §1306.04 (legitimate medical purpose standard); standard of care convention 17
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Single pharmacy

S2-FIXED-SINGLE-PHARMACY

Single pharmacy: I agree to fill all prescriptions for [MEDICATION] at a single pharmacy: [PHARMACY NAME AND LOCATION]. I will notify my provider in advance if I need to use a different pharmacy due to supply issues, travel, or insurance requirements, and will document the reason.

Always included
Authority: Institutional risk management
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Use as prescribed

S2-FIXED-AS-PRESCRIBED

Use as prescribed: I agree to take [MEDICATION] only as prescribed — at the prescribed dose, frequency, and for the prescribed indication. I will not adjust my dose without discussing it with my provider first.

Always included
Authority: Standard prescribing agreement element; CDC 2022 1
Grounded in statute / guideline

No sharing or transfer

S2-FIXED-NO-SHARING

No sharing or transfer: I understand that sharing, selling, or otherwise transferring my controlled substance prescription to another person is a federal felony under 21 USC §841. I agree not to do so.

Always included
Authority: 21 USC §841 19
Grounded in statute / guideline

Safe storage

S2-FIXED-SAFE-STORAGE

Safe storage: I agree to store [MEDICATION] in a secure location, out of reach of children and visitors. I understand that proper storage is my responsibility.

Always included

Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Disclosure of other substances

S2-FIXED-DISCLOSURE

Disclosure: I agree to inform my provider of all other medications, supplements, and substances I am using, including over-the-counter medications, alcohol, cannabis, and any controlled substances prescribed by other providers.

Always included

Drug screening — every 6–12 months

S2-VAR-UDS-STANDARD

Drug screening: I agree to provide urine samples for drug screening when requested by my provider, at a frequency of approximately every 6–12 months or as clinically indicated. I understand that the purpose of this screening is to support safe prescribing, not to punish me.

Included when Risk tier = standard

Drug screening — every visit

Refill timing

S2-VAR-NO-EARLY-REFILLS

Refill timing: I understand that my medication will be prescribed on a set schedule. I agree not to request early refills except in advance for documented travel or documented supply-chain issues. I understand that federal law (21 CFR §1306.12) permits multiple sequential prescriptions with earliest fill dates for up to 90 days of supply when clinically appropriate.

Always included
Authority: 21 CFR §1306.12; CDC 2022 118
Grounded in statute / guideline

Lost or stolen medication

S2-VAR-NO-REPLACEMENT

Lost or stolen medication: I understand that Crystal Run Healthcare's policy is not to replace lost or stolen controlled substance prescriptions. I understand that this is an institutional policy; federal law does not categorically prohibit replacement. If my medication is lost or stolen, I agree to report the loss to my provider and, if stolen, to file a police report.

Always included
Authority: 21 CFR §1306.04; ACOEM Opioid Treatment Contract template; institutional policy 1617
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Extended-release / prodrug formulation

S2-VAR-ER-FORMULATION

Formulation: I understand that my provider has prescribed an extended-release or prodrug formulation of [MEDICATION] because of my history of substance use. I agree not to request a change to an immediate-release formulation without discussing this with my provider.

Included when SUD history = past or active
Authority: ASAM/AAAP 2024 (stimulants); ASAM/ACMT 2025 (benzodiazepines); institutional SUD-aware prescribing standard 23
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Pill counts

S2-VAR-PILL-COUNT-ENHANCED

Pill counts: I agree to bring my medication to any appointment when requested for a pill count. I understand that pill counts may be requested without advance notice.

Included when Risk tier = enhanced or high-risk
Authority: ASAM/AAAP 2024; ASAM/ACMT 2025; institutional enhanced monitoring standard 23
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Treatment goals (function)

S2-VAR-FUNCTIONAL-GOAL

Treatment goals: I understand that the goal of this medication is to support my daily function — my ability to work, maintain relationships, and carry out daily activities — and to manage [CONDITION]. I understand that improvement in function, even if pain or symptoms are not fully eliminated, is a meaningful treatment outcome. I agree to discuss my functional status at each visit.

Always included
Authority: CDC 2022 (functional improvement as primary goal; PEG scale); ASAM/ACMT 2025 12
Grounded in statute / guideline

4Section 4 — Monitoring and Graduated Response

Monitoring is a safety tool, not punishment

S4-FIXED-MONITORING-FRAMING

Monitoring purpose: The monitoring activities described in this agreement — PDMP review, urine drug screening, pill counts, functional assessment — are safety tools, not punitive measures. Unexpected monitoring findings will be discussed with me directly before any clinical decisions are made. My provider will apply clinical judgment to each finding and will not dismiss me from care on the basis of a single monitoring result.

Always included
Authority: CDC 2022 (should not dismiss patients from care on the basis of toxicology results or PDMP information); ASAM/ACMT 2025 12
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Graduated response to concerns

S4-FIXED-GRADUATED-RESPONSE

Response to concerns: If a monitoring finding, pattern of requests, or clinical concern arises, my provider will follow a graduated approach:

(1) First concern: Direct conversation, documentation, and reassessment of the monitoring plan. No automatic change to the prescription.

(2) Repeated or escalating concern: Formal review within the controlled substance agreement framework, urine drug screen if not already done, PDMP review, and discussion of whether the current management plan remains appropriate. Specialist input may be sought.

(3) Significant or unresolved concern: Specialist consultation before continuing. Continuation may not be appropriate without specialist co-management. If I am not able to continue in this practice, my provider will ensure I receive a referral or bridge plan — I will not be abruptly abandoned.

Always included
Authority: CDC 2022; ASAM/ACMT 2025; AAFP (avoid dismissing patients); APS/AAPM 2009 (graduated response to aberrant behaviors) 1246
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Driving & safety-sensitive work

S4-VAR-DRIVING

Driving and safety-sensitive activities: My provider has counseled me that [MEDICATION CLASS] may impair driving and the operation of heavy machinery. I acknowledge this counseling. [IF SAFETY-SENSITIVE OCCUPATION: I understand that my occupation as [OCCUPATION] may have specific regulatory requirements regarding controlled substance use, and I agree to comply with applicable reporting obligations.]

Included when Driving / safety-sensitive = on
Authority: Dassanayake meta-analysis (driving risk); Tran JAMA Network Open 2024 (reporting obligations by state); FAA/FMCSA regulations for safety-sensitive occupations 121314
Proposed institutional standard — no national mandate

Age-related risks (65+)

S4-VAR-AGE-65

Age-related risks: My provider has discussed with me that benzodiazepines and sedating medications carry increased risks of falls, fractures, and cognitive effects in adults over 65, as noted in the American Geriatrics Society Beers Criteria. I understand these risks and have discussed them with my provider. My provider and I have agreed on the clinical rationale for continuing this medication at this time, and will reassess annually or sooner if my functional status changes.

Included when Age ≥ 65 = on
Authority: AGS Beers Criteria 2023; Donnelly PLoS One 2017 (fracture risk); Crowe Arch Clin Neuropsychol 2018 (cognitive effects) 789
Grounded in statute / guideline

Opioid + benzodiazepine combination

S4-VAR-COMBO-OPIOID-BENZO

Opioid + benzodiazepine combination: My provider has informed me that I am prescribed both an opioid and a benzodiazepine. I understand that this combination carries an elevated risk of serious adverse outcomes including overdose. My provider has prescribed naloxone and has counseled my household on its use. I understand that the long-term goal is to reduce the overall burden of these medications, and that specialist input will be sought to plan any changes safely.

Included when Opioid + benzo = on
Authority: FDA boxed warning (2016); CDC 2022 Recommendation 11 (Category B); ASAM/ACMT 2025 (naloxone strong recommendation); Hernandez JAMA Network Open 2018 (time-dependent risk) 121011
Grounded in statute / guideline

Substance-use history

S4-VAR-SUD-AWARE

Substance use history: My provider is aware of my history of substance use and has considered it in prescribing this medication. I understand that my provider has chosen to use [extended-release / prodrug formulation] to reduce misuse risk. I agree to the enhanced monitoring plan described in this agreement. I understand that this agreement reflects my provider's care for my health, not a judgment about my character.

Included when SUD history = past or active
Authority: ASAM/AAAP 2024; ASAM/ACMT 2025; Baweja JAACAP 2025 (outcomes data) 2315

Acknowledgment

S5-FIXED-ACKNOWLEDGMENT

I have read this agreement and have had the opportunity to ask questions. I understand its contents and agree to its terms. I understand that this agreement may be updated at any time with my participation, and that I will be given a copy of any updated agreement to review before signing.

I understand that this agreement is a clinical document, not a legal contract, and that my provider retains clinical discretion at all times.

Always included
Authority: Institutional standard; CDC 2022 1
